Ph.D. Student, School of Computing, KAIST

I am a Ph.D. student in the Scalable Graphics, Vision, and Robotics (SGVR) Lab at KAIST, advised by Prof. Sung-Eui Yoon, with whom I also completed my M.S. I study generative models, particularly diffusion and flow matching, and how to distill them into fast, reliable models. Most recently I have applied this to multi-agent coordination and to consistent text-to-3D generation. I am broadly interested in multimodal models and robot learning.

  • CKAIA 2025

    Rectified Flow Distillation via 2nd Order Inversion for Consistent Text-to-3D Generation

    Sangmin Lee, Taeyeon Kim, Youngju Na, Jumin Lee, and Sung-Eui Yoon

    Shows that stochastic guidance in rectified-flow score distillation gives inconsistent optimization signals, and replaces it with deterministic guidance from second-order ODE inversion for better 3D consistency and finer geometric detail.
